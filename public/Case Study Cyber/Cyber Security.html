How We Made It Happen

Cybersecurity at StradIT is proactive, precise, and battle-tested. This is why took a multi-step approach to help this client to overcome their cybersecurity challenges.

Explore our CoE
We began by understanding everything and carefully mapping each application's architecture, identifying its data sources, and tracing downstream dependencies.
Next, we evaluated the Recovery Point Capability of every application in scope. This included looking at existing backup mechanisms, replay capabilities, and calculating exactly how much data could be lost in different scenarios.
We then designed 100% tailored test plans for each application. Every plan was accounted for in the database type, application criticality, integration complexity, and business function.
Our experts performed tests along with the client teams, ensuring governance standards were met. All evidence, reports, and sign-offs were securely stored for audit readiness.
After testing, we compiled a clear list of gaps and remediation recommendations. For every application, we created or updated playbooks and runbooks with step-by-step instructions for bringing systems back to normal operations with minimal data loss.
These documents became the go-to resource for teams facing an incident.
